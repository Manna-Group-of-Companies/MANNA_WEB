**Why Choose Manna Group for Recycling and Retreading?**

2. **Commitment to Sustainability** — "Retreading a tyre consumes only a fraction of the oil needed to produce a new one, resulting in lower carbon emissions." The retreading process renews worn tyres by replacing tread, reducing demand for new production and conserving materials.

**Conclusion** — Manna Group positions recycling as central to creating a sustainable tyre industry future through retreading services that address waste challenges while offering economic and environmental benefits.
